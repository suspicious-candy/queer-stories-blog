title: "Yusuf’s Questioning Path"
date: "2025-05-02"
excerpt: "Somali-Indian teen exploring identity via podcasts, uncertain of a label."
---

Raised with the call to prayer and Sunday hymns, I learned early that faith could be multifaceted.  
At sixteen, I felt a stir of attraction I couldn’t define and turned to LGBTQ+ podcasts for answers.

Each night, I listened to voices sharing stories of uncertainty—"It’s okay to be unlabeled."  
I typed questions in anonymous forums, hoping someone would say it was normal to just be.

At family gatherings, I answered inquiries about girlfriends with vague smiles.  
I watched cousins pair off, embarrassed but hopeful that my time would come.

Through journaling, I wrote letters to myself—"I don’t know yet, and that’s enough."  
The act of writing became a gentle affirmation of my unfolding self.

One day, I may choose a label.  
Until then, I walk between faiths and feelings with an open heart, learning that identity is a journey, not a destination.
