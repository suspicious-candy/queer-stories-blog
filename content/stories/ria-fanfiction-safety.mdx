title: "Ria’s Fanfiction Haven"
date: "2025-05-02"
excerpt: "Asexual Nepali-American writes fanfic to escape pressure to date."
---

In my Nepali-American household, questions about relationships began early—"Have you met someone special?"  
I felt a disconnect: the curiosity of others versus my own lack of sexual attraction.

Health classes taught everything except asexuality.  
I searched online and discovered the symbol of a black ring, then found a community of asexual fans writing platonic love stories.

I began writing my own fanfiction—chapters celebrating non-sexual intimacy.  
Readers responded with messages like, "This is exactly me," and "Thank you for writing what I feel."

At a writers’ workshop, I published an excerpt anonymously.  
Hearing applause for my chapter on deep friendship felt like validation I’d never received from family.

Now, I share resources about asexuality on my blog and mentor younger teens in online asexual youth groups.  
My stories remind me that love is infinite, and each identity deserves its own narrative.
