---
title: "Imran’s Dual Worlds"
date: "2025-05-02"
excerpt: "Gay Afghan-American balancing faith and identity at home and university."
---

At university, I am Imran—the gender studies major who proudly displays a rainbow pin on my backpack.  
But back home in my Afghan community, I take the name Ali, attend mosque, and speak only of academic achievements.

I spend mornings in lecture halls discussing queer theory, and evenings in video calls with queer Muslims across the globe.  
I record oral histories of LGBTQ+ Afghans and transcribe them in dusty libraries.

Ramadan was a turning point.  
I organized a private iftar for queer friends, where we broke fast with dates and whispered reflections on identity.

In the local mosque, I pray but keep my rainbow pin tucked away.  
I long for a place of worship that welcomes all my selves.

One day, I hope to return home as Imran—fully and without apology—bridging faith and queerness in my community.
