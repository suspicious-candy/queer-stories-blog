---
title: "Devika’s Digital Sanctuary"
date: "2025-05-02"
excerpt: "Pansexual teen in a small town, finding community online."
---

Growing up in a small Indian town meant conformity was a safe path.  
When I came out as pansexual at fifteen, I was met with gossip and ignored pleas for understanding.

I turned to the internet for a lifeline.  
On Tumblr, I found blogs celebrating pan pride; on Discord, I joined private servers filled with queer teens swapping stories and playlists.

My laptop screen became my refuge.  
I decorated my desktop with pride flags and wrote long DMs to online friends who reminded me I was not alone.

At school, bullies mocked my pronoun badge; teachers did nothing.  
I logged off embarrassed, then logged back on determined.

On Discord, I organized a virtual poetry slam for LGBTQ+ youth in conservative areas.  
Dozens of voices rose together in verse about love, fear, and hope.

For the first time, I felt empowered—my identity no longer a secret but a bridge to others.  
I dream of a day when I can walk into my classroom as Devika without hiding behind a screen.
