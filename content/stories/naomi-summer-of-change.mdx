---
title: "Naomi’s Summer of Change"
date: "2025-04-28"
excerpt: "A wilderness camp taught her to shed makeup and breathe."
---

At nineteen, I traveled halfway around the world to a wilderness leadership camp. I thought I’d learn survival skills—building fires, navigating trails—but what I discovered was myself. Our cohort of ten included two trans teens, a nonbinary poet, and a shy lesbian who refused to speak for days.

On the third night, we hiked under a sky unpolluted by city lights. My counselor, Mira, handed me a journal and said, “Write what you’ve never dared to say.” My pen trembled as I wrote:

> “Back home, I wear makeup to blend in. Here, under these stars, I want to take it off and breathe.”

The next morning we held a “share circle” by a lake. When it was my turn, I took off my eyeliner in front of everyone and said, “I’m queer. I’m scared—but I feel alive.” They clapped. They cheered. They welcomed me into their chosen family.

That summer I learned to build shelters and new friendships, to paddle canoes and paddle through internal tides of doubt. I carried a pebble from the lake back home—a reminder that sometimes you need to go far away to find your true self.
